# ☕ POS Cafe Management System (Odoo-Hackathon)

A high-performance, full-stack Point of Sale (POS) solution built for the **Odoo Hackathon**. This system is designed to bridge the gap between intuitive customer service and powerful back-end analytics for modern cafes.

---

## 🌟 Key Modules & Features

### 🖥️ Smart Dashboard
* **Sales Analytics:** Real-time visualization of revenue, orders, and growth metrics.
* **Payment Breakdown:** Categorized tracking of Cash, Card, and UPI transactions.
* **Live Feed:** Monitor recent order activity and kitchen status at a glance.

### 🪑 Floor & Table Management
* **Visual Layouts:** Manage multiple floors (Ground, 1st, 2nd) with a drag-and-drop feel.
* **Status Tracking:** Color-coded tables (Green for Available, Red for Occupied).
* **Table Linking:** Seamlessly assign orders to tables for better table service.

### 🛒 POS Terminal
* **Category Navigation:** Filter by Bakery, Beverages, Coffee, etc.
* **Fast Checkout:** Quick-add items with automatic tax and discount calculations.
* **Stripe Integration:** Secure payment processing with QR code support.

### 👨‍🍳 Kitchen & Staff Management
* **KDS (Kitchen Display System):** Live synchronization of "To Cook" and "Ready" orders.
* **Role-Based Access:** Distinct permissions for Managers, Cashiers, and Kitchen Staff.
